# Viral Shorts Studio: Hindi-to-English Viral Clip Extractor & Editor

An automated viral clipping and video transformation platform that ingests long Hindi-to-English classroom lecture footage, automatically pinpoints high-retention viral comedic moments with killer hooks, and converts them into production-ready 9:16 YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. 

**Core Educational Mission**: Every generated short is strictly engineered as a **Complete Pedagogical Unit** for Indian English learners—guaranteeing at least one concrete educational takeaway (grammar rule, vocabulary, idiom, sentence construction, or pronunciation tip) delivered through high-energy Hindi humor, student laughter, and kinetic on-screen learning cards.

---

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The plan has been revised to enforce the **Educational Delivery Core**: every viral clip must not just entertain, but definitively teach and edify Indian English learners.

- **Mandatory Educational Takeaway (Pedagogical Rule)**: Every viral shot extracted MUST contain a complete, self-contained lesson element:
  1. **Vocabulary / Idiom / Phrasal Verb** with exact Hindi meaning & colloquial Indian context.
  2. **Grammar Rule or Sentence Formula** (e.g. Subject-Verb Agreement with Adverbs: *He always sweeps* vs *I sweep*).
  3. **Natural Spoken English Sentence** that students can immediately adopt in everyday speech.
  4. **Pronunciation & Common Mistakes** (e.g. why *"I loves you"* is broken grammar unless someone is drunk).
- **Lower Canvas Educational Takeaway HUD (Smart Split 9:16)**:
  - Top 52%: Dynamic auto-zoom on the teacher's face, comedic expressions, and gestures.
  - Bottom 48%: Dedicated **Learning Card HUD** showing:
    - 🏷️ **Target English Phrase / Rule** (bold, high-contrast, yellow/white text).
    - 🇮🇳 **Hindi Equivalent** (e.g., राई का पहाड़ बनाना / सिर-पैर न होना).
    - 🗣️ **Example Sentence in Action** (with active word karaoke highlighting).
    - 💡 **Grammar Cheat Sheet Tag** (e.g., `Rule: He/She + Verb(s) | Always = Adverb`).
- **Viral Elements Maintained**: Laughter detection, high pacing, hook banner in first 3 seconds, and audio punchline sound effects (laugh track, ding, whoosh).
- **Duration**: 30 to 45 seconds per short, maximizing retention on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### What It Does
1. **Pedagogical Viral Moment Detector**:
   - Scans lecture videos for segments where an English concept is introduced, illustrated through a hilarious Hindi real-life analogy, and concluded with a memorable English rule or sentence.
2. **Interactive 9:16 Split-Screen Canvas Engine**:
   - Converts standard 16:9 widescreen educational recordings into 9:16 mobile feeds.
   - Synchronizes teacher facial reactions on top with an interactive lesson whiteboard on the bottom.
3. **Sound Effects & Humor Accentuation**:
   - Injects subtle comedic audio cues (laugh track bursts, bell ding on vocabulary reveal, comic whoosh on camera punch cuts) timed to the teacher's delivery.
4. **Hinglish Viral Title & Shorts Publishing Kit**:
   - Generates high-CTR Hinglish titles (e.g., *"शादी में 'Blow out of proportion' का असली मतलब! 😂 | Learn Idioms"*), pinned comment explanation, and search-optimized tags for Indian learners.
5. **Direct Video Exporter**:
   - In-browser Canvas + MediaRecorder export generating `.webm` / `.mp4` 9:16 clips ready to upload directly to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

### Target Audience & Persona
- Indian students preparing for competitive exams (SSC CGL, Bank PO, NDA, CDS), IELTS/TOEFL aspirants, and college students seeking conversational English fluency through entertaining Hindi instruction.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows
1. **Clip Selector with Educational Badges**:
   - The creator views pre-indexed candidate clips labeled by educational type:
     - `[Grammar Rule]`, `[Idiom & Hindi Meaning]`, `[Sentence Pattern]`, `[Pronunciation / Logic]`.
   - Each card displays the Viral Retention Score, Lesson Topic, Hindi Hook, and Target English Phrase.
2. **9:16 Shorts Simulator & Real-Time Player**:
   - Displays a realistic smartphone frame previewing the 9:16 vertical short.
   - Top canvas: Teacher's energetic gestures and facial comedy.
   - Bottom canvas: Live-rendered Lesson Card with Hindi translation, grammar rule pill, and synchronized subtitles.
3. **Studio Customizer & Sound FX Mixer**:
   - Creators can adjust clip trim boundaries (Start/End timecodes).
   - Toggle audio sound effects: `Laughter Track`, `Ding (Rule Reveal)`, `Whoosh (Transition)`.
   - Toggle on-screen elements: `Lesson HUD`, `Hook Banner`, `Subtitles`.
4. **Instant Download & Shorts Kit**:
   - Download the rendered 9:16 video clip.
   - One-click copy for the Title, Hashtags (`#LearnEnglish #EnglishGrammar #Shorts #HindiToEnglish`), Description, and Pinned Comment.

### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Theme**: Dark Creator Studio (`#0B0F19` background, `#111827` panels, `#1E293B` dividers).
- **Accents**: Educational Amber / Golden Saffron (`#F59E0B`) and Punchy Red (`#EF4444`).
- **Typography**:
  - Interface: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - Subtitles & Lesson Banner: Heavy Condensed Sans with black outline and bright yellow/white fill (`font-black tracking-tight`).
  - Timecodes: `JetBrains Mono` / `tabular-nums`.

### Key Modules & Components
1. **`EducationalClipCatalog`**:
   - Structured dataset of pre-indexed viral shots with timestamps, teaching category, grammar rules, Hindi equivalents, and subtitle scripts.
2. **`SmartSplitCompositor`**:
   - HTML5 Canvas dual-crop video renderer:
     - Upper viewport: Focuses on speaker gestures and facial comedy.
     - Lower viewport: Dynamic animated Educational Card showing the target English phrase, Hindi meaning, grammar formula, and active synchronized subtitles.
3. **`SoundFXSynthesizer`**:
   - Web Audio API engine providing zero-latency sound effects (audience chuckle/laughter, vocabulary bell ding, transition whoosh, record scratch).
4. **`CustomVideoUploader`**:
   - File input and drag-and-drop zone allowing the creator to load their own raw video files, with custom in/out trimming and instant AI hook detection.
5. **`MediaRecorderExporter`**:
   - Records the live 9:16 canvas output stream with mixed audio to generate an instant `.webm`/`.mp4` download.
6. **`ShortsPublishingKit`**:
   - Renders ready-to-copy metadata: Click-worthy Hinglish Title, Instagram Reel caption, 15 YouTube tags, and timestamped educational description.

1. **Step 1: Backend Setup**:
   - Set up Express server with `/api/gemini/shorts-kit` using `@google/genai` (`gemini-3.8-flash`) for automated title generation and grammar extraction.
2. **Step 2: Smart Split 9:16 Canvas Compositor**:
   - Build dual-camera canvas cropping (teacher portrait on top, lesson slide on bottom) with dynamic zoom and layout toggles.
